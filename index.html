Can You Fix the Broken Line Tool?

A geometry program draws a line through two selected points using the slope formula. It works for some point pairs but fails for others. Can you identify what the rule is missing?

ByLinwei YuThe University of Hong Kong

THE GEOMETRY CHALLENGE

One rule. Two different results.

A student-built line tool uses the slope formula. Compare what happens when the tool receives these two pairs of points.

m = (y₂ − y₁) / (x₂ − x₁)
✓   WORKSA (1, 2) · B (3, 6)

The program draws the line.   y = 2x

!   ERRORA (1, 2) · B (1, 6)

The program cannot calculate the slope.   No line is drawn.

?
THE CHALLENGE

Find out why the program fails. Develop a rule that can draw the correct line through any two distinct points and recognize when the two selected points coincide.

03

Represent the findings

Explain

Clarify the mathematical conditions

Organize the observations into three cases. Explain when the slope formula is valid and when the two points determine a unique line.

CASE A
x₁ ≠ x₂
Nonvertical line · Slope defined
CASE B
x₁ = x₂, y₁ ≠ y₂
Vertical line · Slope undefined
CASE C
x₁ = x₂, y₁ = y₂
Coincident points · No unique line
Slope defined
m = (6 − 2) / (3 − 1) = 2

The unique line is y = 2x.

01 · MATHEMATICAL UNDERSTANDING
Explanation of mathematical conditions
Distinguish nonvertical, vertical, and coincident cases, and explain when the slope formula is defined.


02 · CT PRACTICES
Use of computational reasoning
Identify relevant information, represent cases, develop conditional rules, and test and debug solutions.


03 · SOLUTION QUALITY
Correctness and scope of the rule
Check that the rule handles all three cases and explain why the conditions cover every pair of input points.




Evidence: Students’ observations, explanations, proposed rules, test cases, and revisions.
